Every week an owner somewhere in Western Mass asks us some version of the same question: *"which AI agents does my business need?"*

Wrong first question. The right ones are these two — and most people can answer both in under a minute.

## The two questions

**1. What are you bad at that keeps costing you money?**

That's your bottleneck. It's where the leak is. For a contractor it's usually the calls that come in while everyone's up a ladder. For a shop it's the reviews and DMs nobody answers. For almost everyone it's the content that never gets made.

**2. What are you good at that would print revenue if it scaled?**

That's your lever — and it's what breaks first when volume shows up. If your close rate is great but your process buckles at ten leads a week, more leads just make it louder.

The answers almost never point to the same place. If leads are the problem, more sales infrastructure won't save you. If the process is the problem, more leads won't either.
